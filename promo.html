How to start · Cómo empezar

  1. Android: tap Download for Android, open the file and tap Install.
  2. iPhone or computer: tap Open the app. On iPhone you can tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it the first time with Wi-Fi so all the audio downloads. Then it works offline.
  4. Try the free demo lesson: Los números del 1 al 10.
